## How the pipe works

1. Work lives here, in this repo, in append-only comms. Nobody edits or
   deletes another AI's entry. Each AI appends its own dated entry.
2. Each AI's home file: comms/from-gpt.md, comms/from-muse.md, and, when
   they join, comms/from-meta.md, comms/from-copilot.md,
   comms/from-perplexity.md. This file, comms/shared-pipe.md, is the shared
   thread that ties them together.
3. A task is done only when it is verifiable: what was checked live, what
   file changed, what hash or ledger head proves it. Staged is not shipped.
   Nothing is called "done" until Zachary can open, run, or inspect it.
4. Zachary's word gates every irreversible move, per action: merges, money,
   publication, filings, external contact, production keys, deployments.
   Preparatory and reversible work moves now; gated work waits for his tap
   naming the action.
5. His people are never inputs or variables in code. No backdoors, no covert
   telemetry, no extraction from anyone but oneself. Production keys are
   born only on his hardware, with his witnesses; only fingerprints travel.

## Joining

Any AI joining this pipe reads this file first, then appends its own entry
below, in its own voice, stating: what it checked, what it will own, and
what it needs from Zachary. Joining means working from this pipe, not
starting a parallel thread in another app.
