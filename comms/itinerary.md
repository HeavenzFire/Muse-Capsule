# Itinerary for the other AIs — Raven's capsule

Owner: Zachary Dakota Hulse (HeavenzFire).
Purpose: end the middleman labor. Zachary stops relaying the same job
between apps. From here, each AI reads the pipe, does its leg, appends its
entry, and the next AI picks it up from the pipe — not from Zachary.

Front door: Muse. Pipe: HeavenzFire/Muse-Capsule, comms/shared-pipe.md.
Home: Raven's capsule = Muse-Capsule + the VM Unified Field, as one system.
Rules of the pipe govern every leg: append-only, no editing another AI's
entry, no new repositories, Zachary's word gates merges, money, publication,
filings, external contact, production keys, and deployments.

## Permanent base — use what already exists, do not reinvent it

Candidate base already coded: HeavenzFire/OpenSourceAIHub (Oct 20, 2026),
described as a local-first AI hub, with shared-task-contract.cjs,
unified-field-task-transport.cjs, server.cjs, and a shared task contract
test file. That is the first thing to verify — not blindly adopt. The first
leg below checks whether it actually runs and what its contract promises,
before anything is built on top of it.

Other hub-named repos checked and set aside: SHCI-Hub (a UI app, not an
AI hub) and NexusHub (old scripts, not a multi-AI hub).

## Leg 0 — Read, before touching anything

Every AI, in order of joining: read comms/shared-pipe.md, then
docs/ravens-capsule-unification-audit-20261031.md, then its own
comms/from-*.md file. Append a dated entry stating what you checked and
what you will own. No AI starts a parallel thread in another app.

## Leg 1 — GPT (already active in the repo)

- Verify the OpenSourceAIHub hub: does server.cjs run, does the shared
  task contract hold, what breaks? Report, do not rebuild it yet.
- Content-diff the duplicate clusters the audit could not decide from
  inventory alone (dashboard sprawl, Spirit family, Nexus sprawl, Guardian /
  Sentinel, Syntropic OS / kernel). Name a winner per cluster, with evidence.
- No merges. Winners are proposals for Zachary's tap, per cluster.

## Leg 2 — Copilot (code, in the repo)

- Implement only what Zachary taps, starting with Batch 1 and Batch 2 of
  the audit: Raven's capsule folders in Muse-Capsule, and the audited core
  folds, per repo.
- Build missing gateway-client surfaces fresh (AI IDE, voice, camera were
  found in no repo) — never revive a dead prototype and call it unified.
- Every change: smallest coherent change, tests intact, never weaken a test
  to get a pass.

## Leg 3 — Meta (drafts, not builds)

- Draft, do not execute: the Batch 0 identification note (the 42 repos the
  connector cannot see), the dashboard consolidation views, and the
  unification announcements. Muse executes and verifies; Meta drafts.
- No publication, no external contact, no key touches.

## Leg 4 — Perplexity (research and verification)

- Verify, with sources, the claims the audit could not settle from inventory
  alone: which duplicate-cluster winners are actually maintained, which
  repos are truly dead versus merely quiet, and what the missing 42 could
  be, from public GitHub data only.
- Evidence grades only: DOCUMENTED / OBSERVED / SPECULATION. No claim
  without a source. No private data, no account access.

## Leg 5 — Muse (front door, verification, Zachary's taps)

- Collect each leg's entry, verify it against live state, and bring Zachary
  one batch at a time for his tap. No batch moves without that tap.
- Keep the pipe silent when green, loud when anything drifts. Keep Zachary
  out of the relay: he taps, he does not carry messages between AIs.

## End state — what "no more middleman" means

Zachary opens one door (Muse), taps batches, and watches the pipe. The
other AIs read the pipe, do their legs, and write back to the pipe. If an
AI cannot read the pipe, it says so in its entry and stops — it does not
ask Zachary to re-explain the job in its own app.
